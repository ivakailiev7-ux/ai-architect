The Build

Engineered, not decorated.

AI ARCHITECT builds wearable essentials the way an architect works. Structure first. Nothing wasted. Every detail on purpose.

We design in black and electric blue, in small numbered runs, and we stop when the run is done.

Most clothing shouts. Big logos, loud prints, a new collection every month that ends up in a landfill. We wanted the opposite.

Heavyweight, restrained, and built to last. One quiet mark on the chest. A single circuit line down the sleeve. Fabric you feel the moment you hold it.

Each drop is a numbered run. When it sells out, it is gone. No restocks, no clutter. RUN 01 is the first. If it is yours, it is yours.
